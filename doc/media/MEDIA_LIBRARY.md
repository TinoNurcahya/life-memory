## Prompt dasar video

Gunakan gaya bersama ini pada semua prompt: **quiet documentary lifestyle video, editorial cinematic realism, subtle film grain, natural imperfect motion, warm natural light, no text, no logo, no watermark, no identifiable faces, seamless loop, 10 seconds**.

1. **Café rain alternate** — Rain slowly trails down a café window while a warm lamp reflection shifts almost imperceptibly; notebook and coffee remain still in the foreground; locked camera, gentle focus breathing, seamless loop.
2. **Steam from a cup** — A thin ribbon of steam rises from a ceramic cup on a wooden table, changing shape naturally; soft morning window light, close macro framing, minimal camera movement, seamless loop.
3. **Afternoon curtain** — A sheer curtain moves lightly in an open bedroom window, sunlight drifting across rumpled linen and a closed book; slow static shot, calm afternoon atmosphere, seamless loop.
4. **Train window** — A quiet train moves past a rain-speckled window, trees and houses becoming soft horizontal motion outside, warm interior reflection on the glass; no passengers visible, steady camera.
5. **Notebook pages** — Hands slowly turn two blank notebook pages beside a pencil and coffee; only hands visible, no readable writing, close overhead framing, natural page movement, seamless loop.
6. **Street after rain** — Reflections of street lamps shimmer in shallow puddles on a quiet residential road after rain; a distant shop glows softly, locked low camera, no people, seamless loop.
7. **Two empty chairs** — Two empty chairs outside a quiet café move slightly as a breeze passes through nearby leaves; late afternoon light, restrained environmental motion, locked camera, seamless loop.
8. **Night desk** — Monitor glow and a warm desk lamp illuminate an unfinished project; shadows from leaves move slowly across the wall, no readable screen text, locked camera, seamless loop.
9. **Morning kitchen light** — Dawn light gradually crosses a kitchen table with one cup and folded cloth; dust motes barely visible, no people, slow static composition, seamless loop.
10. **Walking feet on wet pavement** — Two pairs of feet walk slowly across wet pavement, reflections stretching beneath them; frame stays below the waist, documentary handheld feel, loopable ending.
